The Rich History

Agra, once the capital of the Mughal Empire, is home to architectural marvels like the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, and Fatehpur Sikri. Its legacy is etched in sandstone and marble.

Top Attractions

From the majestic Taj Mahal to the bustling Sadar Bazaar, Agra offers a blend of heritage and vibrant street life that captivates every traveler.

Local Culture

Agra’s culture is a fusion of Mughal traditions, local crafts, and delicious cuisine. Petha, leather goods, and zardozi embroidery are local specialties.
